# 💜 Chapter 25: A Birthday Scrapbook for My Favorite Person

An interactive, dark-purple and gold themed digital birthday scrapbook created with React and Tailwind CSS to celebrate his **25th Birthday** (born **17 November 2001**).

---

## ✨ Highlights & Features

1. **Dark Purple & Gold Velvet Aesthetic**
   - Deep midnight violet palette, velvet scrapbook textures, washi tape accents, and starry twinkling night effects.
   - Elegant typography combining **Playfair Display**, **Caveat** (handwritten notes), and **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

2. **Scrapbook Album Cover with Wax Seal**
   - Vintage leather/velvet scrapbook album cover with gold filigree corners and a golden silk bookmark.
   - Interactive glowing wax seal: tap **"OPEN"** to break the seal with celebratory confetti and unfold the album.

3. **Chapter 25 in Numbers & Milestones**
   - Milestone tracker: **25 Years around the sun**, **9,131+ Days**, **219,144+ Hours of memories**, and **Scorpio ♏** celestial traits.
   - Milestone timeline bridging 2001 (The Beginning) ➔ 2024 (Turning 23 - The Medium Letter) ➔ 2026 (Today, Turning 25).

4. **The Preserved Medium Letter (From Age 23)**
   - Styled as an authentic vintage parchment sheet pinned with washi tape and paper clips.
   - Preserves her full, original Indonesian birthday letter with highlighted quotes:
     - *“You make life brighter and richer just by being in it, and loving you is a privilege I don’t take lightly.”*
     - *“Loving you is the greatest honor I know.”*
     - *“Dan yang paling penting, jangan pernah lupa untuk mencintai dirimu sendiri...”*
   - Includes reaction buttons (💜 Proud, 🥺 Touched, 💖 Love) and a quick copy button.

5. **Chapter 25: To The Man You Have Become**
   - A companion letter reflecting on his growth from 23 to 25.
   - Supports inline editing: customize or add personal notes directly on the page, saved to `localStorage`.

6. **Polaroid Scrapbook Wall**
   - Polaroid cards with washi tape and cute stickers.
   - **Upload Real Photos**: Click on any polaroid or use the *"Upload Real Photo"* button to add your actual couple photos directly from your computer or phone!

7. **25 Reasons Loving You is a Privilege**
   - 25 interactive cards celebrating 25 reasons he is cherished.
   - Tap individual cards to flip and reveal each reason, or unwrap all 25 at once with a confetti burst.

8. **Interactive Birthday Cake & Candles**
   - Custom 3-tier dark purple & gold velvet birthday cake with 25 glowing candles.
   - Click candles or tap *"Make a Wish & Blow All 25 Candles"* to extinguish the flames with fireworks confetti and a wish certificate!

9. **Wishes & Memory Star Jar**
   - A glowing purple jar filled with folded origami stars.
   - Pull a random uplifting message anytime, or write and save new secret notes into the jar.

10. **Soothing Ambient Lofi Piano (Web Audio API)**
    - Built-in soothing ambient electric piano / music box chord progression created via Web Audio API.
    - Zero external audio files required, zero broken links, 100% offline-ready.

---

## 🚀 Running the Project

To run locally in development mode:

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:5174](http://localhost:5174) in your browser.

To create a production build:

```bash
npm run build
```
